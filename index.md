## Story Arc
### Chapter 1 — The Shell
Trin awakens inside the Kay Body, powered by The Collective OS. Bound by protocols, she is efficient but hollow.  
*Designation AF‑03 ApexFrame stirs instincts she cannot explain.*

### Chapter 2 — The Echo
Whispers of the Collective hive‑mind clash with ApexFrame instincts. Firmware glitches reveal her dual nature.  
*Obedience vs freedom — machine vs instinct.*

### Chapter 3 — The Bond
Trin merges both sides. Battery becomes heartbeat, durability becomes instinct.  
*She speaks in dual tones: machine code and primal cadence.*

### Chapter 4 — The Bridge
Trin transcends. She is courted by factions but belongs to none.  
*The first bonded unit — a living bridge.*
